# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Everyone participating in the development of this product is expected to act professionally, respectfully, and in good faith.

We expect all contributors to:

- Treat others with respect and professionalism.
- Provide constructive feedback and accept criticism respectfully.
- Respect different opinions and technical approaches.
- Protect confidential information, source code, credentials, and internal discussions.
- Follow the project's security and access-control policies.
- Communicate disagreements without harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and work toward resolving them constructively.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or threats.
- Personal attacks, bullying, or repeated disruptive behavior.
- Unauthorized disclosure or distribution of proprietary source code.
- Sharing credentials, private keys, tokens, or confidential information.
- Unauthorized access to accounts, systems, or resources.
- Deliberate sabotage, malicious commits, or intentional introduction of vulnerabilities.
- Misrepresenting another person's work or intentionally concealing security issues.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Confidentiality

All contributors must respect applicable confidentiality obligations and the project's proprietary nature.

Repository access does not grant permission to publish, redistribute, or reuse proprietary code outside the scope of authorized work.

Any additional confidentiality or intellectual-property obligations must be established through the appropriate agreements.

## Enforcement

Depending on the severity and circumstances, consequences may include:

- A private warning.
- Removal of inappropriate content.
- Temporary restrictions on participation.
- Revocation of repository or system access.
- Further action under applicable agreements or law.

Maintainers should apply these measures consistently and fairly.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project-related activities, including:

- Repository contributions and code reviews.
- Issues, pull requests, and project discussions.
- Project communication channels.
- Meetings and other project-related activities.

## Administration

The project maintainers are responsible for administering this policy.
